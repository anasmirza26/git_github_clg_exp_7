About Premier League

The Premier League, founded in 1992, is the top tier of English football. It is known for its competitiveness, world-class players, historic clubs, and passionate fans. Each year, 20 clubs compete for the title, European qualification, and to avoid relegation. The winners receive the prestigious Premier League trophy, while the top clubs also compete in the UEFA Champions League.

Famous Stadiums

Old Trafford

Home of Manchester United, known as the "Theatre of Dreams". Capacity: 74,000.

Anfield

Liverpool's home ground, famous for its atmosphere and "You'll Never Walk Alone". Capacity: 54,000.

Etihad Stadium

Manchester City’s modern home, symbol of their rise in world football. Capacity: 53,000.

Stamford Bridge

Chelsea’s historic ground located in London. Capacity: 40,000.

Emirates Stadium

Home of Arsenal, a modern ground in London. Capacity: 60,000.
